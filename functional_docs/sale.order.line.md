**Summary:**

<!-- SUMMARY:model:sale.order.line -->
Adds repair line behaviour without new fields. When a line belongs to a RUG-confirmed repair order, create/write resets its unit price to the product cost and keeps the original price in `x_studio_price_unit_original` (port of Studio automation 193). An onchange on product, quantity and price marks lines as re-estimated and sets their counter while the repair order is unlocked for re-estimation (port of Studio automation 204).
