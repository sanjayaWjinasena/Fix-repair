**Summary:**

<!-- SUMMARY:model:account.move -->
Adds the RUG (warranty) invoice handling for repair sale orders. Is Rug Invoice (`is_rug_invoice`) and Is Rug Account Set (`is_rug_account_set`) are computed flags. The "Change to RUG Account" button (`action_change_to_rug_account`) moves every product line to the company's RUG account from Repair Accounts (`x_repair_accounts`) and sets RUG Account Updated (`x_studio_rug_acc_updated`). On posting a RUG invoice, `_rug_auto_settle` creates a clearing entry (DR RUG account / CR Debtors) and reconciles it, so the invoice becomes paid without Register Payment. `_get_view` hides Register Payment on RUG invoices, hides Post until the RUG account is set, and makes the form read-only once it is posted. The `write` override (port of Studio automation 215) settles the invoice again when RUG Account Updated is set on an invoice that is already posted, and raises an error if the linked sale order is not locked.
