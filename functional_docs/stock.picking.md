**Summary:**

<!-- SUMMARY:model:stock.picking -->
Links transfers to repair tickets through Helpdesk Ticket (`x_studio_helpdesk_ticket_id`) and Created from Help Ticket. These fields show on the transfer form and feed the ticket's Movements button. Adds former Studio computes (Fully Paid SO, Repair Payment Made, Cash Full Payment Made, FSM Task Done, Valid Factory Repair, Valid Transfer Lines, User Location Validation, Need Approval) and quotation-type fields. Validate is hidden, and refused in `button_validate`, on customer-pays repair transfers until the sales order has a paid or partly paid invoice (`nuw_block_validate`). When a warranty repair delivery is done, `_action_done` moves the linked ticket to the next repair stage.
