**Summary:**

<!-- SUMMARY:model:account.payment -->
Adds the related flags Is Rug Invoice (`is_rug_invoice`) and Is Rug Account Set (`is_rug_account_set`), read from the payment's journal entry. Adds an onchange, `_fix_repair_onchange_validate_advance_pct` (port of Studio automation 241 "RR - Validate Payment %"). On a draft payment for a Repair-type sales order, it raises an error when the posted payments plus this payment stay below the company's advance-payment percentage of the order total. It covers payments entered directly on the payment form, outside the Register Payment wizard.
