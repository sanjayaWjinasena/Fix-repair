**Summary:**

<!-- SUMMARY:model:res.partner -->
Adds 12 fields to contacts in three groups. Bank guarantee: Bank Guarantee Docs and its filename, and Mandatory Bank Guarantee. Vendor: Vendor Account (`x_studio_vendor_account`), with related Vendor Name and Address. Tax registration: VAT Registration Status, Number and Exempted Number, VAT Registered, and SVAT Registration Status and Number. They are placed into the contact form groups created by BugFix-Sales. A second view (`view_partner_form_hide_dev_extras`) hides unused contact form elements such as the membership page and some smart buttons.
<!-- /SUMMARY -->
