**Summary:**

<!-- SUMMARY:model:sale.order -->
Extends sales orders to handle repair quotations. Not Under Warranty (`x_repair_customer_pays`) marks customer-pays repairs. Approve RUG and Reject RUG buttons (`action_approve_rug_direct`, `action_reject_rug_direct`) sit next to Request RUG Approval. The Re-estimate button (`action_re_estimate`) returns the order to draft, clears the signature and moves the ticket back to Diagnosis. A red "Not Enough Stock" warning (`x_repair_stock_ok`, `action_show_stock_shortage`) checks availability in the factory resupply locations. The Create Invoice button is redirected to `action_repair_create_invoice`, which creates one full invoice for non-RUG repairs. Stored computes from former Studio fields (Overdue, Over Credit and its amount, Over Bank Guarantee and its amount, Over Commission, Guarantee Status) feed the confirm gates. On create, the quotation type is set automatically for repair orders. Writes move the linked ticket's stage and track the lock / re-estimate status.
